FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers to the questions clients ask the most.

How long does a design take?

Most social media posts and posters are ready in 1 to 2 days. Logos can take 3 to 5 days.

How many changes can I ask for?

Each package includes free revisions. Extra changes after that can be done for a small extra charge.

What do I need to send you?

Send your text, your logo (if you have one), your brand colors and any ideas or examples you like.

How do I pay?

Contact me and I will share the payment details. Payment methods include JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank transfer.

Which file formats will I receive?

You will get PNG or JPG files for posts, and print-ready PDF files for posters.

Can you design for my small business?

Yes! I love working with small businesses and helping them look professional online.
